Monthly bookkeeping

The everyday work of keeping your books accurate, done every month so you never fall behind again.

this is the one most people start with

What you get

  • Every transaction categorized and bank and card accounts reconciled
  • Your one-page plain-English report by the 10th of each month
  • A standing 20-minute call to walk through it, if you want one
  • Answers to quick questions by email or text, no meter running

A good fit if

  • You're spending evenings on bookkeeping instead of your business
  • You have software but don't trust what it's telling you
  • You want to know where the money went without learning accounting

Catch-up and cleanup

Behind by a few months or a few years? We pick up the pieces, fix what's tangled, and bring you current.

no judgment. truly.

What you get

  • A review of where things stand and a fixed quote before we start
  • Missing months rebuilt from bank statements and receipts
  • Miscategorized and duplicate entries found and corrected
  • A clean starting point for monthly bookkeeping or your CPA

A good fit if

  • Tax season is coming and your books aren't ready
  • A previous bookkeeper left things in a mess
  • You've been meaning to "get to it" for a while

Payroll and contractor support

People get paid right and on time, and the paperwork that follows gets handled.

nobody likes a payroll surprise

What you get

  • Payroll set up and run through Gusto

A good fit if

Tax-time handoff

your CPA will thank you

What you get

A good fit if

Questions people ask

Do I need accounting software?

It helps, but you don't need to have it already. We'll recommend something that fits your size, usually QuickBooks Online, or NetSuite or Sage Intacct for growing companies, set it up, and show you the two or three things you'll actually use.

Do you do taxes?

We're bookkeepers, not a CPA firm, so we don't file tax returns or give tax advice. We keep your books ready for tax time, work directly with your CPA, and can introduce you to one we trust.

How far behind is too far?

We haven't found it yet. Whether it's three months or three years, we'll look at what you have and give you a fixed quote to get caught up.

Is my financial information safe?

Yes. We use read-only bank connections where possible, secure cloud software with two-factor sign-in, and never ask you to email passwords or full account numbers.

I'm local. Can we meet in person?

Absolutely. Clients around Nashville can meet at their business or anywhere convenient, and we're happy to collect paper receipts.

Can I switch from my current bookkeeper?

Yes, and it's easier than it sounds. We'll handle the handoff, review what's there, and let you know if anything needs fixing first.

Not sure which one you need?

That's what the free call is for. Tell us what's going on and we'll point you to the right fit, even if it isn't us.
