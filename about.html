Why "Layman's Ledger"?

Because the people who run small businesses shouldn't need an accounting degree to understand their own money.

Most small business owners we meet are great at what they do: baking, building, fixing, designing, teaching. Bookkeeping isn't that. It shows up as a pile of receipts, a bank feed full of question marks, and a report from the accountant that might as well be in another language.

We started Layman's Ledger to fix the second half of that problem. Plenty of people can keep your books. Our job is to keep them and explain them, in the words you'd use yourself, so you can make decisions with confidence instead of guessing.

That means no jargon, no mystery invoices, and no question too basic. If you've ever nodded along in a meeting about your own finances, we built this for you.
