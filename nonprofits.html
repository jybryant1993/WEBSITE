What we do for nonprofits

Everything a small finance team would handle, without hiring one.

  • Fund accounting and grant tracking

    Restricted and unrestricted money kept separate, every grant tracked against its budget, and funder reports ready before they're due.

  • Board-ready reports

    Monthly or quarterly financials your treasurer and board can read in five minutes, with the questions they'll ask already answered.

  • Audit and review prep

    Books closed, schedules built, and documents organized for your auditor. We work with them directly so your staff doesn't have to.

  • Form 990 support

    The financial statements, functional expense breakdown, and schedules your 990 needs, ready for your CPA or tax preparer to file.

  • Budgets and cash planning

    Annual budgets built with your team, tracked against actuals, and a clear view of your cash through the lean months between grants.

  • Systems that fit nonprofits

    QuickBooks Online set up for fund accounting, or Sage Intacct when you outgrow it, with your data cleaned up on the way in.

Nonprofit questions

Do you work with small or new nonprofits?

Yes. Many of the organizations we help run on a small staff or a volunteer treasurer. We'll set up books that work at your size and grow with you.

Do you file our Form 990?

We prepare everything the 990 needs (financial statements, the functional expense breakdown, and supporting schedules) and work with your CPA or tax preparer on the filing.

Can you work with our auditor?

Absolutely. We'll prepare the schedules and documents they request, answer their questions directly, and keep your staff out of the back-and-forth.

We have a volunteer treasurer. Can you work alongside them?

Of course. We handle the day-to-day books and give your treasurer clear, plain-English reports so their role stays manageable.

What software do nonprofits need?

Most small nonprofits do well with QuickBooks Online set up properly for fund accounting. As you grow, Sage Intacct is built for nonprofit reporting, and we can move you when it makes sense.
