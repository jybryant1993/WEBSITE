FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to know accounting to work with you?

Not at all — that's the whole point. We handle the technical side and explain everything in everyday language.

What software do you use?

We work with popular cloud platforms like QuickBooks Online and Xero. If you're not set up yet, we'll help you choose and configure one.

I'm months behind on my books. Can you help?

Yes. Our catch-up & cleanup service gets your records current, then we keep them that way going forward.

Do you file taxes?

We prepare clean, organized, tax-ready books and coordinate directly with your CPA or tax preparer so filing is fast and accurate.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Plans are month-to-month. Annual billing is optional and saves you 10%.
