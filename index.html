Everything we do, in detail

How it works

It starts with clean books and grows from there. The first step costs nothing.

  1. A free 30-minute call

    Tell us about your business and where your books stand. We'll listen, ask a few questions, and give you an honest read, even if we're not the right fit.

  2. We get your books in order

    We connect to your bank and software, clean up anything from the past, and set up a system that runs without you chasing it.

  3. A report you'll actually read

    Every month: one page, plain English, and a quick call if you want to talk it through. Questions in between? Just text or email.

  4. Advice when the big calls come

    As you grow, we help you plan: forecasts, pricing, financing, new systems, and whether now's the time to hire. Same person, same plain English.
